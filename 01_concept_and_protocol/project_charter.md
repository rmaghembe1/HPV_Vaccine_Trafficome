# Project Charter — HPV Vaccine Trafficome

## Central question
How do developmental age, vaccine regimen/formulation, baseline immune state, sex, metabolic-inflammatory state, and health context remodel intracellular antigen handling and APC–adaptive immune coupling after HPV vaccination?

## Primary mechanistic hierarchy
1. VLP uptake and endolysosomal handling
2. HLA class II processing/loading and APC activation
3. Tfh–B-cell–plasmablast differentiation and durable antibody memory
4. Fc-receptor engagement and phagocyte communication
5. Sterol/membrane-state, UPR, and inflammatory regulation
6. HLA class I/cross-presentation and immunoproteasome pathways as context-dependent secondary mechanisms

## Feasibility rule
Do not infer participant adiposity from pathway signatures. BMI/adiposity analyses require measured participant-level metadata. Cross-vaccine datasets provide mechanistic triangulation, not surrogate HPV outcomes.
