# Phase 2C2A Fiji cross-reactive breadth

## Decision

**READY_FOR_PHASE2C2B_CROSSREACTIVE_BREADTH_INFERENCE**

- Cross-reactive HPV types: HPV31, HPV33, HPV45, HPV52 and HPV58
- Shared systems-serology features: 11
- Mechanistic axes: binding-antibody abundance, IgG-subclass architecture, Fc-receptor communication and global shared serology
- Participant-level breadth rows: 640

Breadth is represented by the mean, median, minimum, positive-antigen count and twofold-antigen count across the five cross-reactive HPV types. Raw and matched BPV-calibrated scores are retained separately. The BPV-calibrated scores provide the preferred basis for HPV-associated cross-reactive interpretation.
