# Phase 1E Fiji immunization-context correction

## Decision

**PASS**

## Biological correction

Participants with zero previous 4vHPV doses were HPV-vaccine-naive at Visit 1. Their Visit 2 measurements therefore represent a primary response to 2vHPV, not a booster-memory or recall response.

Participants with one, two or three previous 4vHPV doses represent a distinct memory trajectory. Visit 1 captures immunity approximately six years after their last 4vHPV dose, whereas Visit 2 captures heterologous recall 28 days after 2vHPV administration.

## Authoritative contexts

| Previous 4vHPV doses | Visit | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | v1 | Unvaccinated pre-2vHPV baseline |
| 0 | v2 | Primary 2vHPV response at day 28 |
| 1–3 | v1 | Six-year persistence after 4vHPV |
| 1–3 | v2 | Heterologous 2vHPV recall at day 28 |

## Statistical consequence

The visit effect must be interpreted conditionally on previous 4vHPV exposure. The visit-by-dose interaction distinguishes primary immune induction from memory persistence and recall and is therefore biologically more informative than a uniform Visit 2 booster effect.

## Supersession

This report supersedes earlier uniform descriptions of all Visit 1 observations as persistence and all Visit 2 observations as recall. Numerical results from Phases 1C and 1D remain valid.
